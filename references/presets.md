# Preset selection

Choose the smallest useful setup. Presets are editable guidance. Leave creator/reader unset when the request gives no relevant identity or audience. Inspect only a selected entry with `python3 scripts/quillume.py show KIND ID`.

## Writing angles

| ID | Name | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| rewrite | Clear rewrites | Keep the original point and qualifications while replacing clutter with a short, readable draft. |
| expand | Expanded posts | Develop a short note into a connected post, with enough context to explain the reasoning. |
| deep-dive | Deep dives | Explore a decision and its implications in a substantial piece that keeps the evidence and limits visible. |
| thread | Social threads | Turn one source into a short sequence of posts, each making a useful contribution to the whole. |
| contrast | Before-and-after contrasts | Show how a generic claim becomes specific when it names the actual decision, process and boundaries. |
| opinion | Opinion posts | State an arguable position clearly and support it with the facts in the source, without manufacturing controversy. |
| story | Short stories | Give supplied events a clear situation, tension and next step while keeping hypothetical scenes labeled. |
| explain | Beginner explanations | Introduce an idea through three key points and a labeled analogy, with the depth adjusted to the reader. |
| reply | Useful replies | Respond to the actual point with a specific observation and a useful next step, without generic agreement. |
| takeaways | Numbered takeaways | Arrange the source into a short numbered list of concrete points the reader can scan and use. |

## Editing methods

| ID | Name | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| clear-rewrite | Clear, faithful rewrite | Rewrite the source in clear, natural English. |
| concise-rewrite | Cut the clutter | Shorten the source by roughly one third where possible without removing essential qualifications, decisions or evidence. |
| voice-match | Match my creator voice | Rewrite the source using the selected creator’s tone, sentence rhythm, preferred structure and boundaries. |
| beginner-friendly | Explain for a beginner | Rewrite for a reader who is new to the topic. |
| expert-brief | Write an expert brief | Turn the source into a concise brief for an experienced reader. |
| founder-update | Shape a founder update | Organize the source as a founder update with short sections: What changed, Why it matters, and What comes next. |
| source-story | Tell a grounded story | Reshape the source into a short narrative: the situation, the tension, the change and the takeaway. |
| balanced-argument | Build a balanced argument | State the source’s main position clearly, organize its supporting reasons, and preserve its strongest qualification or counterpoint. |
| scannable | Make it easy to scan | Rewrite for scanning on a phone. |
| reader-adaptation | Adapt for this reader | Rewrite the source for the selected reader’s background, goals and content preferences. |
| professional-email | Turn notes into an email | Turn the source into a professional email with a specific subject and concise body. |
| claim-aware | Review and rewrite carefully | First provide a revised version that preserves only source-supported claims. |

## Refinement tools

| ID | Name | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| humanize | Plain-language edit | Rewrite plainly and naturally. Remove clichés and filler; preserve facts and meaning. |
| titles | Title ideas | Generate 10 English title candidates. Explain each hook briefly. Avoid unsupported claims. |
| check | Writing review | Review clarity, factual support, specificity, tone, repetition and unsupported claims. Give actionable edits. Do not claim to determine AI authorship. |
| x | X thread | Adapt into an X thread with posts under 280 characters separated by ---. |
| reddit | Reddit post | Adapt into a useful Reddit post with a title, context and concrete detail. Avoid promotion. |
| quora | Quora answer | Adapt into a direct, helpful Quora answer. |
| blog | Blog article | Adapt into a blog article with a title, introduction and clear sections. |
